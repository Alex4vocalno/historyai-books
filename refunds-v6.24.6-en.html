Refund Policy

Earlier policy · Originally effective: 2026-09-10

This is historical text, not the current policy. Saving text does not establish acceptance by any user.

1. How to request a refund

Email support@evoronai.com with your account email, order ID, payment date, pack, requested amount and reason. You may include a payment receipt with sensitive details redacted. Do not send full card numbers, passwords, verification codes or payment keys. Explain if someone else made the payment so it can be checked securely.

2. Unused and partly used credits

There is currently no promised universal change-of-mind refund window. You may request a refund of unused credits. We review the order, delivery, account usage and applicable law before responding. Requests involving partial use can also be submitted; eligibility and amount depend on the findings and applicable rights, not an automatic full refund or balance-based refund.

3. Duplicate charges, missing credits and failures

After verifying a duplicate payment or delivery error, we coordinate credit correction, delivery or a payment-channel refund according to the circumstances and applicable rights. Check the original order before paying again for missing credits. Failed tasks, abnormal retries and suspected duplicate usage charges are reviewed against actual usage and the failure, not solely task status or whether a finished manuscript exists.

4. Refund method and credit adjustments

Approved refunds are processed through the original payment channel. Posting time depends on payment providers and banks and is not guaranteed to be immediate. A full refund reverses the order’s credits; a partial refund adjusts them in proportion to the refunded order amount, rounded to whole credits. If credits have already been consumed, the balance may become negative and remaining entitlements need review; this does not automatically trigger an additional card charge.

5. Promotional credits and account actions

Promotional credits without a corresponding cash payment do not create a cash refund. Deleting a manuscript, archiving a project, signing out or requesting privacy deletion does not automatically refund a payment. A refund does not ensure removal of already published or downloaded content. Credit corrections and cash refunds are explained separately; a credit grant is not a cash refund.

6. Consumer rights and payment-provider handling

This policy does not limit statutory withdrawal, refund, repair or other remedies. Starting generation, using credits or accepting general terms does not by itself waive a statutory withdrawal right. Where a transaction legally requires separate consent to immediate performance and acknowledgement of rights, that must be obtained separately. Rules differ by region and service classification; we do not apply an “all digital products are non-refundable” rule.

Creem or another relevant payment institution may handle disputes or refunds under its rules and applicable law. You can use official support channels on your receipt; our product support is not the only route for a lawful complaint or dispute.
