How credits work

Credits are for writing and editing, not a reading subscription. Writing, processing materials, editing and retries may consume credits. Charges are not based on the word count of the finished manuscript. Usage varies by project; packs do not guarantee a fixed number of completed books.

Before you buy

  • Paid creation accounts and writing credit purchases are for users aged 18 or older. This purchase requirement does not restrict free reading.
  • Purchased credits currently have no automatic expiry. Promotional credits are not cash-redeemable.
  • Model use incurred before failure or cancellation may consume credits. Billing errors and failures can be reviewed.
  • Archiving or deleting a manuscript does not automatically reverse usage. Generated results still require your review.
