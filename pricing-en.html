How credits work

Credits are deducted based on actual use during creation. Writing, processing materials, editing and retries may consume credits. Charges are not based on the word count of the finished manuscript. Usage varies by project; packs do not guarantee a fixed number of completed books.

Delivery and refunds

Credits are added to the purchasing account after server-side payment confirmation. Contact support for duplicate charges, missing credits or service failures. See the Terms of Service for refunds; applicable consumer rights remain unaffected.
