How credits work

Credits are deducted for service usage, including model usage. Context, generation, editing and retries can all consume usage. Credits are not billed per manuscript word and a pack does not guarantee a particular number of finished books. Historical API cost is an internal cost reference, not a cash exchange rate for credits.

Delivery and refunds

Credits are added to the purchasing account after server-side payment confirmation. Contact support for duplicate charges, missing credits or service failures. See the Terms of Service for refunds; applicable consumer rights remain unaffected.
