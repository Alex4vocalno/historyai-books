Privacy Policy

Historical text captured 2026-09-09; original page updated 2026-09-08.

This is historical text, not the current policy. Saving text does not establish acceptance by any user.

1. Scope and contact

This policy covers the EVORON AI writing studio, ebook library and account services. Contact the support email below for privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete your information.

2. Information we process

We process account names, email addresses, pen names and login information; conversations, writing settings, materials, manuscripts and covers you submit; reading progress, shelves and interactions such as reviews; and orders, credit records, model usage, operational and security logs. Do not submit unnecessary sensitive personal information in prompts or manuscripts.

3. How information is used

We use this information to provide writing and reading services, save progress, process payments and credits, troubleshoot issues, prevent abuse and respond to support requests. Private manuscripts are not automatically published to the library.

4. Model, payment and infrastructure providers

To perform requested generation tasks, relevant prompts, materials and context are sent to OpenRouter and the selected model providers. Hosting, email and backup providers may process data needed to deliver the service. For Creem payments, Creem and its payment partners process checkout and payment information; we receive order and payment status and do not collect full card numbers through the studio. Providers also process data under their own terms and policies.

5. Public publishing

When you choose public publishing or post public reviews, that content, your pen name, cover and book information can be accessed and shared by others or indexed by search engines. Withdrawal does not automatically remove copies already saved by third parties.

6. Storage, retention and international processing

We retain information as needed to provide the service, recover backups, maintain security and meet applicable obligations. Deletion requests require account verification; legally required transaction records and backups awaiting rotation may not be removed immediately. Servers and providers may be outside your country or region. Contact support for applicable retention and international processing arrangements.

7. Cookies and local storage

Login sessions use cookies. Language, reading preferences and progress features use browser local storage. Clearing these may sign you out or reset local preferences.

8. Your requests and policy updates

You can contact support to request access, correction, export or deletion of account-related information and exercise other rights available under applicable law. We may request information necessary to verify your identity. Policy updates are dated on this page.
