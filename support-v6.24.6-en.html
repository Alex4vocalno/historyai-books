Contact Support

Earlier policy · Originally effective: 2026-09-10

This is historical text, not the current policy. Saving text does not establish acceptance by any user.

1. Contact email

Email support@evoronai.com. Include “Account”, “Payment/refund”, “Book”, “Privacy” or “Content report” in the subject to help route the request. This is product support, not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services for immediate danger.

2. Account and usability issues

Provide your account email, time, page URL, steps and necessary screenshots. You can contact us by email even when you cannot sign in. Redact personal information and credentials from screenshots; support does not need your password, verification code or payment key.

3. Purchases and credits

Provide the order ID, payment date, pack and issue. For missing credits, check the original order rather than paying repeatedly. For usage questions, include the book, time and relevant records; do not send a whole private manuscript. See the Refund Policy for conditions and handling.

4. Response time

We provide an initial response within 3 business days of receiving a support email. An initial response is not completion of an investigation, refund approval or bank settlement. We explain next steps when more information or time is needed. Shorter deadlines required by applicable law remain applicable.

5. Privacy and content requests

For privacy requests, specify the scope of access, correction, export or deletion. For copyright or other content reports, provide public links, the rights basis or specific risk. To appeal, refer to the original action and explain your reasons. We may verify identity, request more information or limit submission of sensitive materials.

6. Following up

Keep your email and order reference and reply in the same email thread when adding information. Investigation, refund approval and bank posting are separate stages. Lack of an immediate reply does not mean approval. Requests with statutory response or processing deadlines are handled under the applicable requirements.
