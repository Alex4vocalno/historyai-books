1. Content screening before image generation

When image content screening is enabled, relevant text prompts for covers and illustrations are sent to the Creem Moderation API before they reach the image model. Screened text may include your book title, pen name, scene description, revision instructions and context excerpts needed for generation. This is a separate purpose from payment processing through Creem. Screening requests use a random request identifier and do not separately attach your login email, account identifier, payment details or complete manuscript. Do not include unnecessary personal or confidential information in prompts.

To troubleshoot screening and verify its execution, a private audit record stores the time, random request identifier, screening environment, decision and provider result identifier. This dedicated record does not store the prompt text, generated image or full provider response. This does not mean other writing or operational records contain no content, or that Creem does not retain the prompts it receives. Provider processing and retention depend on its own terms and applicable arrangements. Contact support@evoronai.com for screening-related data questions or to challenge a decision.

2. Purchase age confirmation

When you purchase writing credits, we record your declaration that you are at least 18 and the server-recorded confirmation time with the order. This step does not collect your birth date or identity documents and is not identity or age verification. We do not backfill this declaration for older orders or treat it as waiving statutory rights or consenting to other data uses.

3. Scope and contact

This policy covers the ebook library, writing studio, account and support services provided under the EvoronAI brand. “We” means the operator of this service. EvoronAI is a brand name, not a representation of company registration or a particular place of incorporation. Contact support@evoronai.com for privacy matters. This policy does not replace third-party website policies or remove our applicable data-protection obligations.

4. Account and contact information

When you register, sign in or contact support, we process your email, pen name, account identifier, password-verification data, sessions, email-verification and password-reset records. Necessary information identifies your account, verifies actions and restores access. A pen name may be public; your login email is not automatically published as author information. Do not send support passwords, verification codes or full card details.

5. Writing materials and model processing

We process conversations, book settings, attachments, references, drafts, cover prompts and generated results to perform writing, editing, cover and export requests and save progress. Relevant content and context are sent to OpenRouter and the providers processing the requests; research requests may also send search terms to search services. Processing is not necessarily confined to our website servers.

Providers may have different rules for logging, retention, training and safety review. We do not promise zero retention or no training across all models. Do not upload materials you lack permission to process, confidential documents subject to restrictions, or unnecessary sensitive personal information. Contact support before submitting material with specific processing requirements.

6. Reading and public content

Shelves, bookmarks, reading progress, preferences and reviews support reading and synchronization; some preferences are stored in your browser. Text, titles, covers, descriptions, pen names and reviews you choose to publish may be accessed, shared, downloaded or indexed by search engines. Review personal information and third-party rights before publishing. Withdrawal cannot retrieve copies already saved by others.

7. Payments, credits and transaction records

We process order identifiers, packs, currency, payment and refund status, credit changes and usage records to deliver credits, reconcile accounts and resolve disputes. Creem and its payment partners handle checkout and card information for Creem purchases; the studio does not collect full card numbers. Payment records may be retained for financial, fraud-prevention or legal requirements separately from manuscript deletion.

8. Operational, security and support data

Servers may record access times, network addresses, browser request information, actions, errors, model usage and support correspondence to operate the service, prevent abuse, reconcile usage and troubleshoot. Diagnostic records may contain content relevant to an issue. Access is limited according to the task; encryption, access controls and backups cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Providers and other recipients

In addition to model, search and payment providers, hosting, email, storage and backup providers process data necessary for the service. Necessary information may also be disclosed to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal requests, protect users and the service, or handle complaints. Readers are recipients of content you choose to make public.

10. International processing and applicable rights

Servers and providers may be outside your country or region, where privacy rules can differ. Hosting in Hong Kong does not mean all processing occurs there or that every jurisdiction’s data laws are automatically satisfied. Where applicable law requires transfer safeguards, a particular legal basis or additional consent, these depend on the processing and applicable arrangements. Contact support for information relevant to your request.

11. Retention, deletion and backups

Accounts and manuscripts support ongoing access, saving and recovery. Transaction records support delivery, refunds, disputes and applicable financial duties. Operational, security and support records support troubleshooting, abuse prevention and request handling. Retention depends on purpose, account status, outstanding matters and legal obligations; signing out is not a deletion request.

Privacy deletion requires identity verification and scope confirmation. Working copies, published works, search caches and backups are distinct. Deleting something in the interface does not immediately erase every backup. When data must remain, we explain the categories, reasons and available handling arrangements in the request response. Archiving a manuscript is not account deletion or withdrawal from publication.

12. Cookies and local storage

Login cookies maintain and protect sessions. Browser storage supports language, reading preferences, progress and interface state. You can manage or remove this information through the browser, which may sign you out or remove local settings. Storage on third-party checkout pages is subject to their policies. Accepting service terms is not consent to non-essential marketing, advertising tracking or additional training uses.

13. Making a privacy request

Email support@evoronai.com with your account email, request type and scope to request access, correction, export or deletion. We may require identity checks proportionate to the request, but not your password. Applicable law may also provide rights to object, restrict processing, withdraw consent, portability, appeal or complain to an authority; applicability depends on your circumstances.

We handle requests within applicable deadlines and explain necessary extensions, inability to fulfill a request or lawful retention. Withdrawing consent does not affect lawful processing based on consent before withdrawal. A privacy request does not automatically cancel an order or refund a payment; you can submit the corresponding payment request as well.

14. Minors and policy changes

This service is not designed for children. Do not submit children’s personal information without a lawful basis. Contact support if you believe it has been improperly collected. Eligibility remains subject to the terms and applicable law; guardian consent alone does not establish that every form of children’s data processing is lawful.

This page identifies the policy version and dates and retains historical text. Material changes are communicated through appropriate product notices or contact channels. Uses requiring separate consent are not authorized merely by updating this page.
