Acceptable Use Policy

Text version: v6.21.0
Historical text captured 2026-09-09; original page updated 2026-09-08.

This is historical text, not the current policy. Saving text does not establish acceptance by any user.

1. Scope

This policy applies to conversations, input materials, manuscripts, covers, reviews and public publishing. AI generation does not mean content is fact-checked, rights-cleared or endorsed by the platform.

2. No unlawful or infringing use

Do not use the service for fraud, phishing, scams, impersonation, privacy violations or intellectual property infringement. Do not bulk-copy or redistribute unauthorized books or fabricate authorship, sources or endorsements.

3. No harmful content

Do not generate or distribute child sexual exploitation, pornography, non-consensual intimate imagery, targeted harassment or threats, incitement to hatred or violence, or operational instructions facilitating real-world harm or illegal activity. Do not use cover generation to create deceptive impersonations of real people.

4. Responsible writing and reader protection

Lawful historical, cultural, literary and critical discussion is allowed, but cannot be used to evade these restrictions. Do not present generated content as professional medical, legal or investment advice, or distribute unverified AI drafts as genuine testimony or authoritative findings. Do not provide academic cheating or fabricated research services.

5. System safety

Do not bypass content safety, account or billing controls, steal credentials, attack the service, send spam or abuse resources through automation.

6. Reporting and enforcement

Report infringing or harmful content to support with a book link, description and relevant evidence. We may investigate, restrict access or remove content; affected users may appeal through the same email. These rules do not mean all generated content has been manually reviewed.
