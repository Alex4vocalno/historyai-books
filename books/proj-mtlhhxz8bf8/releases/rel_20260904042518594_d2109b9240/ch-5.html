噩梦与录音

The acrid scent of smoke still clung to the， lining of her nostrils, a phantom residue， from the Lowell house. Nora sat in her car,， parked a block from her own apartment, engine， off, hands gripping the steering wheel as if， it could anchor her. On her phone screen,， the photos she’d taken last night glowed with， a cold, digital clarity: the severed, corroded， gas line in the basement, its jagged end， nothing like the ‘probable electrical fault，’ cited in the official report. And next to， it, a close-up of a faint, sticky residue on， a kitchen cabinet—a residue her flashlight， had caught w。

The residue on the cabinet, a dull amber smear， in the high-resolution image, was the final， piece. It wasn’t grease. It wasn’t varnish.， It was, she was almost certain, the same， industrial adhesive she’d seen used in a， hundred construction sites, the kind that， came in a two-part epoxy syringe and set like， rock. The kind you’d use to temporarily seal， something, or to secure something you didn’t， want found. The official report had no mention， of it. No samples taken.。

She exhaled, a shaky breath that fogged the， cold windshield. Her knuckles ached from， their grip. The logical part of her brain,， the part trained in forensic documentation， and chain of custody, screamed that this was， nothing. A coincidence. An unrelated repair，. The emotional part, the part that still， smelled smoke and woke gasping at 3 a.m.,， whispered that logic had failed the Lowells.。

She had to move. Sitting here, staring, was， its own kind of paralysis. Nora shoved the， phone into her coat pocket, the phantom weight， of it against her thigh feeling heavier than it was.。
